# Architecture review, 2026-09-07

An original, locally trained spatial generative model with learned field dynamics is feasible on this Apple M4 Pro with 24 GB unified memory. A short local training run cannot substantiate Genie 3 parity. Explicit graphics rendering, scene persistence, editing, and branching are useful product capabilities, but are established ideas, not three demonstrated scientific breakthroughs. Publish the original trained weights, training recipes, held-out measurements, renderer provenance, and scope limits.

## Primary evidence and important recent prior art

- [GameNGen, April 2025 revision](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.14837v2): training used 128 TPU-v5e devices for 700,000 steps and 70 million image examples at 320x240. Its one-million-example ablation could render novel viewpoints but struggled with game logic. This is a concrete reason to avoid promising broad neural-video behavior from a small laptop run. Its conditions/noise augmentation are useful lessons for reducing autoregressive drift.
- [DIAMOND](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.12399) and [Delta-IRIS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.19320) show narrow environments support compact useful learned simulators. The latter encodes change and reports substantially faster training than earlier attention-based models.
- [InfiniteDiffusion, revised May 3 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08309): learned infinite terrain already has seed consistency, constant-time random access, hierarchical diffusion, and a public implementation. Persistent terrain or a learned replacement for Perlin noise cannot be presented as unprecedented.
- [Modeling What Changes, September 2 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02046): per-object change gates plus residual dynamics report 2.5 to 4.6 times better pose accuracy and 8.6 to 11.1 times fewer parameters than a dense MLP on a narrow MuJoCo benchmark. Planner success remains 0.23 plus or minus 0.06 after planner-relevant training. This is promising evidence for efficiency, with a substantial gap between accurate next states and successful control. Code release is promised upon publication, not confirmed available.
- [MRO-GWM, June 1 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01950): action-conditioned rigid-object dynamics over canonical Gaussian splats with a spatiotemporal transformer. Combining an explicit 3D representation and learned dynamics is established prior art.
- [LPWM, March 4 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04553): object-centric stochastic dynamics learned from videos, with action, language, and image-goal conditioning. The paper links code/data/models and reports ICLR 2026 Oral acceptance.
- [Better Slots, August 12 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12078): a controlled study finds well-bound object representations improve robustness, while competitive pretrained scene features show that the benefit should not all be attributed to object decomposition.
- [WorldExam, August 3 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02603): 1,474 cases and eight tasks separate visual quality, controls, spatial consistency, and world reactions. Its 20-model study finds these abilities do not reliably co-occur.
- [PlayWorld, August 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13552): 171 objective-based scenarios assess geometry, interaction and out-of-view evolution. Fixed identical keypress sequences can be unfair across models with different controls; compare successful tasks and equivalent paths instead.
- [Decision-making evaluation position, June 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15032): visual fidelity does not establish planning utility. Test interventions, closed-loop rollouts, policy rankings, and uncertainty if making those claims.

## Proposed honest implementation

Train a small conditional rectified-flow network to generate a two-channel spatial field: height and vegetation density. Three biome labels provide explicit conditioning. Use original deterministic synthetic terrain as training data, preserve disjoint seeds for validation, and ship weights and measured flow loss. A browser renderer turns those learned fields into a navigable scene with terrain, water, vegetation, lighting and atmosphere. State exactly which details are neural and which are rendered or procedural.

Train a separate residual convolutional model to predict water, vegetation and heat after rain and heating controls. The data generator supplies original analytic training trajectories. At runtime the neural network predicts the field updates, without calling that analytic teacher. Evaluate held-out single-step error, long rollouts, and a persistence baseline. Describe it as a learned synthetic ecosystem, not learned general physics.

Three falsifiable product targets are exact persistence across a return journey/reload, reproducible saved branches with localized edits, and interactive rendering on the laptop without a paid inference service. Measure state checksums, branch isolation and latency separately from model quality. None of these, by themselves, establishes scientific novelty or Genie 3 parity. A substantive next research step would add real terrain/scene data with audited licenses, broader object interactions, and external evaluation.
